# Saved companies and annual comparison

Saved companies use browser local storage under `cds_saved_companies_v1`. All 484 searchable entries can be saved, removed and reopened without an account. Invalid stored IDs are ignored. When storage is blocked, the list remains usable during the visit. The top Saved button opens a dedicated page showing all saved companies. Both that button and the front-page Saved tab display the saved count, such as Saved (0) or Saved (1), and update together. The Saved tab on the front page still filters its company list and clears the search and sector filter when selected. Both views share the same saved list and comparison controls.

Compare supports up to three of the 482 companies with matched annual records. TSMC and SpaceX retain their original report pages and can be saved, but have no matched annual record in this snapshot. The other eight original profiles have separate annual records available for comparison; their original pages retain their own reporting periods.

The comparison table shows both fiscal periods, the named revenue and income definitions, and the supplied financial measures. Charts can show revenue growth, income/revenue or operating cash. Signed bars use a shared scale; missing values are not drawn as measured zero. Cross-sector selections identify the differing business models. No forecast or company ranking is assigned.

The Compare page has three company search boxes. Enter two companies and optionally a third, use the built-in suggestions if needed, then select Compare or press Enter. The form checks that every supplied entry resolves to a different supported company before replacing the current selection. The charts, selection chips and financial table show the same two or three companies. Clearing Company 3 returns the comparison to two companies. Unknown, ambiguous and unavailable entries leave the existing results intact and show an inline message. Comparison cards selected from the directory continue to work.

`web/explore.js` manages saved state and comparison selection. `web/compare.js` renders the selected annual data. Both read the existing SQL-generated catalog. They add no industry filters, annual-signal filters, sorting or browser CSV export. The six company tabs, original ten profiles, additional Deep Analysis and source links remain intact. The top navigation has no preview badge or New search button. Saved and Compare sit beside the company logo; selecting the CDS brand returns to company search.

Automated checks cover saving and removal, persisted and blocked storage, stale IDs, the three-company limit, unavailable annual data, fiscal periods, chart measures, report navigation, signed values and safe text rendering. The navigation and comparison flow also receive a browser review for release. Changes to the main branch deploy through the Pages workflow after hosting is configured.
