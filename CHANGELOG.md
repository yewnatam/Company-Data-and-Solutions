## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-29

Initial release of Company Data and Solutions.

- Search 484 supported company profiles by name or ticker.
- Browse companies by sector and save them in the browser.
- Compare two or three companies using matched annual reporting periods.
- Keep Saved and Compare in the top navigation; return to search with the CDS brand.
- Read Overview, Financials, Markets, Insights, Deep Analysis and Sources.
- Trace reported results to company publications and saved SEC data.
- Rebuild the static website through a Python and SQLite pipeline.
- Run automated data and interface checks before publishing.

The data is a saved reporting snapshot. This release does not refresh company
results or market prices automatically. Company-level recommendations are
analysis, not statements from management.
